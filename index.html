Who We Are

Baptist Union Sierra Leone is a fellowship of believers committed to the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Rooted in the Baptist tradition that began in Sierra Leone in 1961, we exist to make disciples, strengthen families, and serve our communities in Sierra Leone and beyond.

Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or looking for a church home, you are welcome here. We gather to worship God, study His Word, and care for one another.

Ministries

Sunday Worship

Biblical preaching, prayer, and congregational singing every Sunday morning. Come as you are.

Children & Youth

Age-graded teaching, games, and discipleship so the next generation can know and follow Jesus.

Community Care

Practical support, prayer, and outreach that reflects Christ’s love in Freetown neighbourhoods.
