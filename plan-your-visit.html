What to Expect

  • Arrival Greeters will welcome you at the entrance and help you find a seat or the children’s check-in area.
  • Dress Come as you are. Most people dress smart-casual; some wear traditional attire. There is no dress code.
  • Service length The main worship service typically lasts about 90 minutes and includes singing, prayer, Scripture reading, and a sermon.
  • Language Services are primarily in English, with occasional use of Krio. Everyone is welcome.
  • Giving Offering is collected during the service. You may also give online at any time.

Children’s Ministry

We want children to feel safe, known, and taught the Bible at their level. During the 10:30 AM worship service we offer:

  • Nursery For infants and toddlers. Staffed by screened volunteers in a dedicated room.
  • Children’s Church For children of primary-school age. Bible teaching, songs, and activities in a separate space while parents attend the main service.
  • Check-in Arrive a few minutes early. Our team will register your child and show you the rooms. You may stay with your child until they are settled.

Parents remain responsible for their children before and after the programme. If you have questions about ages or special needs, please contact us before you come.
